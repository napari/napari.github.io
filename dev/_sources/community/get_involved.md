# Get involved

There are many ways to be a part of the napari community. From being a code or
documentation contributor, to creating educational content or examples, or
just sharing how you use napari, you are welcome to help develop and improve
napari.

## Chat and meet with us

The best place to ask questions and interact with the team is our
[Zulip community chat](https://napari.zulipchat.com/). You can also join one
of our regular [community meetings](meeting-schedule), or dive deeper on a
particular topic with one of our [working groups](napari-working-groups).

To ask general questions about napari usage, including plugins, you can visit
the [image.sc forum](https://forum.image.sc/tag/napari).

## Contributing and bug reporting

napari development happens in the napari GitHub repository
[github.com/napari/napari](https://github.com/napari/napari). If you spot a
bug, please check our [issues list](https://github.com/napari/napari/issues).
If you do not find a matching report, please open a new issue.

If you are interested in contributing, check out our
[contributing guide](napari-contributing).

## News and updates

To read announcements, learn more about who is using napari and see what our
community has to say, check out our blog, the
[Island Dispatch](https://napari.org/island-dispatch). To get the latest news
from the napari team, follow us on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/napari),
[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/napari.org), or
[Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@napari).
